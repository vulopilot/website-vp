Questions

Pricing, plainly.

Do I need Pro to use VuloPilot?

No. The free plugin scans your whole site — SEO, AI visibility, performance, security, accessibility, content and (with WooCommerce) commerce — and gives every area a score. Pro adds deeper checks and automation on top of what free already does.

How does the per-site pricing work?

One license key activates Pro on the number of sites in your plan — 1, 3, or 10. Pick the tier that matches how many WordPress sites you manage.

What happens if my license expires?

Pro features stop working until you renew, but your scan history, settings and data all stay exactly as they were. Nothing is deleted.

Can I turn off Pro features I don't need?

Yes. Every Pro capability is its own module under Settings → Modules — turn on only what you use.
