FAQ

Questions people ask before they start.

Will VuloPilot change my site without asking?+

No. VuloPilot shows you the issue and a suggested fix. Supported AI fixes are applied only when you choose, and account or server changes stay with your admin or developer.

Does it guarantee better rankings or AI citations?+

No tool can promise that. VuloPilot strengthens the signals that support discovery — clearer titles, better answers, faster pages — which is what actually helps over time.

Do I need to be technical to use it?+

Not for the Free plan. Findings are written in plain language, and AI tools help draft the fix. Some advanced items are better handled by a developer, and we'll tell you when that's the case.

What's included in the 100 free AI credits?

They cover AI Writer, Blog Generator and the Duplicate Content finder — enough to try real fixes on your site before deciding on Pro.
