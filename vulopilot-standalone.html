06 · FAQ

Quick answers.

What exactly is VuloPilot?+

A WordPress plugin. It scans your site, tells you what's wrong in plain language, and helps you fix it — with AI assist where that's useful.

Will it change my site without asking?+

No. VuloPilot shows you the issue and a suggested fix. You approve every change before anything goes live.

Do I need to be technical to use it?+

No. Every finding is written in plain English, with a clear next step. Some advanced issues are better handled by a developer, and we'll tell you when that's the case.

Does it guarantee better rankings?+

No tool can promise that. VuloPilot fixes the things that are known to help — clear titles, useful content, fast pages — which is what actually moves the needle over time.
